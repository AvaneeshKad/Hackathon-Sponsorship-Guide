# Hackathon-Sponsorship
This repo has all you need to get your hackathon sponsorship

## Start

The first thing you do is **Plan**.

Set up the Google Drive/Onedrive/Cloud Storage/Self-hosted Folder that contains :
- Sponsor Tracking Sheet
- Previous Contacts
- Team Member info
- Meeting Minutes
- Sponsorship Deck

Management is the key to a successful organizing team.

#### *Speaking of management : Lets talk about **Time Management***

There is only one thing to remember : Do it ASAP!

But here's one caveat - Dont rush without preparation. Otherwise, you would get nothing but disappointment.

## Preparation

### Discussing with the team
  - What's the goal?
  - Who are we?
  - What does our hackathon/event offer?
  - How can we add more value and incentives for the corporate-companies/startups/sponsors?
  - Timeframe of the event
  - Venue
  - Sponsors Online or Offline
  - How is the Judging process going to be?
  - The Sponsorship Tiers
  - Flexibility in the tiers

### Sponsorship Deck

Making the sponsorship deck is not a light job. 
It takes many iterations, changes, and removal of extra stuff.

Having a sponsorship deck at the start helps build an image of the hackathon that is crucial for the company to remember your org as a unique identity.

What the deck should include :

 **Primary** :
 
  - Introduction to your Hackathon - with a punch line that is a selling point for your event.
  - Sponsorship Tier Table - Everything should be visible properly(Do not make it cluttered!)
  - Flexibilty options/Separate Tracks/Special Funds Option
  - Contact info
  - Past Hackathon photos

  **Add-ons** : 

  - Featured Projects
  - Different Ideas
  - Custom Hackathon Tracks 
  - Past Data / Statistics (if any)
  - Recommendation/ Positive Comments from faculy/professor/student-orgs/judges/mentors/past-sponsors

The sponsorship deck should have a nice theme/graphics.
Can add a mascot (if possible)

**The Important part is having a sponsorship deck that is enthusiastic but professional at the same time.**

## Sponsorship Tiers

Divide the things you offer in 3 parts 

- Talent Acquisition
- Engagement
- Brand Presence
